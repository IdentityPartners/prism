Headless creation — Bluesky

Bluesky is the one platform with an open account-creation API, so Argentica can create the account for you end-to-end. You choose the handle, email and password; the account is created and recorded in the kit below.

Signup kit — every platform

For platforms without open signup APIs, the kit holds everything for a two-minute manual signup: the direct signup link, what verification that platform demands, your generated profile ready to paste, and a status you control. Platforms with their own posting APIs are marked — once a profile exists, the Post tab can publish to it.
