Integration Guide
Booking Widget — Embed on identitypartners.uk

Option 1: Prism Booking Page (recommended)

Add this to your website's </body> tag:

<script src="https://prism.identitypartners.uk/chatbot/embed.js"
  data-type="booking"
  data-position="bottom-right"
  data-color="#0f3b3a"
  data-label="Book an Initial Consultation">
</script>

Option 2: Zoho Bookings Embed

Once Zoho Calendar is connected, embed your booking page directly:

<iframe src="https://bookings.zoho.eu/portal/identitypartners"
  width="100%" height="600" frameborder="0">
</iframe>

Option 3: Setmore

Setmore is the simplest option. Sign up at setmore.com, connect to Zoho Calendar, then embed:

<script id="setmore_script" type="text/javascript"
  src="https://storage.googleapis.com/fullintegration-live/webComponentAppListing/Container/setmoreIframeLive.js">
</script>
<a id="Setmore_button_iframe" style="float:none" href="https://booking.setmore.com/scheduleappointment/YOUR_KEY">
  <img border="none" src="https://storage.googleapis.com/full-assets/setmore/images/1.0/Logo/book-now-blue.svg">
</a>

Zoho Calendar Integration

Zoho Bookings integrates directly with Zoho Calendar. Once you connect Zoho Calendar in Prism:

  1. Go to bookings.zoho.eu
  2. Create a service: "Initial Consultation — 20 minutes — Free"
  3. Connect to your Zoho Calendar
  4. Copy the embed code
  5. Paste into identitypartners.uk

Bookings will appear automatically in your Prism Calendar page.
