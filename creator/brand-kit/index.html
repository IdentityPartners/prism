Usage Guidelines
✓ DO
Use Deep Teal as the primary accent on light backgrounds
Use Playfair Display for all headings and editorial content
Maintain generous whitespace — the brand breathes
Use warm ivory (#f7f3e9) as the primary background
Write in British English throughout
Use the full tagline on formal materials
✗ DO NOT
Use the logo on busy photographic backgrounds
Stretch or distort the logo
Use colours outside the approved palette
Use sycophantic or AI-trope language in copy
Use American English spellings
Use more than two typefaces in a single design
Voice & Tone
Brand Voice
Warm but professional. We care deeply about the people we work with, and it shows — without being saccharine.
Evidence-based. We cite sources. We label inferences. We do not invent statistics.
Direct. We say what we mean. We do not hedge unnecessarily or apologise for having a point of view.
Human. We write like people, not institutions. Short sentences. Active voice. Real words.
Words We Use / Avoid
Use: recovery, wellbeing, community, support, evidence, practice, journey, growth
Avoid: delve, tapestry, landscape (metaphorical), synergy, holistic (overused), journey (overused), empower (overused)
Never: "Great question!", "Absolutely!", sycophantic openers, closing motivational platitudes
